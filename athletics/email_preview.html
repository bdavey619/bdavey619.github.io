State of Play

Late-inning rallies are the only currency that matters in a rebuild, and Muncy just reminded this team how to spend it.

The Athletics held the lead early, lost it in the fifth when Boston scored twice, and spent four innings tied before Muncy broke the seal in the ninth. This is not a comeback story. It is a team that held what it had, stayed close when it mattered, and found the one swing that turned a game they could have lost into a game they won. Ginn's six scoreless innings gave them the foundation, but the real test was whether the lineup could stay patient enough to get another chance. They did. Muncy delivered when the tie game demanded a separator. The bullpen protected it. Alvarado and Harris combining for two clean innings after Basso held the fifth. This is what a two-run win looks like when nothing comes easy: pitching holds the door, the lineup stays alive long enough to find an opening, and one hitter closes it. They are now 2-1 in their last three games, and for a team 12.5 back, every night like this one counts double.
