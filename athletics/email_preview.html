State of Play

The game was decided in the first inning. Chicago scored six before the Athletics recorded an out.

What to Watch

Down eight runs by the end of the first inning, there was no comeback window to miss. The game was forfeit before most lineups were even warm. This is what inconsistency looks like when it turns into predictability. The starting pitcher failed fast, the offense failed to respond, and the bullpen had nothing to fix. They have now lost nine straight. The problem is not one thing. It is everything holding at once.
