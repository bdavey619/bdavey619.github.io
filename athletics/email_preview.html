State of Play

Washington's pitching line forced Oakland into chase mode early, and the bullpen collapse made it clear. The game was decided by inning five.

The Athletics never led and never recovered after the third inning, when Washington scored three to take control. Soderstrom's solo home run in the ninth was the only moment that mattered offensively. One swing in a night of fourteen strikeouts and ten runners left on base. The rest of the lineup couldn't convert. Six relievers cycled through after Jump exited after 3.2 innings, and three of them gave up six runs apiece: Sterner, Tur, and Cortes each contributed to a bullpen implosion that turned a one-run deficit into a blowout. This is what happens when early damage compounds without relief. The pitching broke first, the offense never arrived, and by the time Soderstrom hit his solo shot, it was noise against a 19-run margin. Inconsistency has been the season's spine, but this was collapse by committee.

What to Watch

Zack Littell takes the ball against J.T. Ginn tonight at 7:05 PT, and the Athletics need to avoid the early spiral that buried them yesterday. Littell sits at a 4.90 ERA. Hittable if Oakland's lineup shows discipline at the plate instead of swinging through another night of empty at-bats.
