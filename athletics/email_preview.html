State of Play

Tommy White sparked a three-run eighth, but it wasn't enough to overcome early deficits that haunted them throughout.

They trailed from the first inning through the end. Henry Bolte drove in three to keep them relevant, and White's eighth-inning single started a rally that brought them within one. But they never led. They left nineteen runners on base across nine innings and converted only four into runs. The problem wasn't a lack of contact. Fifteen hits is respectable offensive volume. But rather timing and leverage. In the sixth inning, with two runners in scoring position, they managed nothing. That's where the margin disappeared. The pitching held long enough for a chance to exist, but the offense needed to be sharper in those critical moments, and it wasn't. Inconsistency delivered again.

What to Watch

Keider Montero takes the mound for Detroit tonight with a 3.34 ERA. The Athletics need to find something early before the game gets away like it did yesterday.
