State of Play

Civale gave them a clean five innings. The lineup turned it into a 6-2 win, and now they're three straight.

They broke it open early and never let Baltimore back in. Civale didn't give an inch. Six hits, zero runs across five frames, and six strikeouts to match. Which meant the bats only had to do enough. Rooker delivered that enough, driving in three runs and finding one over the fence. The real moment came in the third when they pieced together three runs and the game stopped being a question. Baltimore scored twice in the eighth but it landed on a lead that was already five. This is what a controlled game looks like: early damage, pitching that holds, and a margin that was never going to shrink back to nothing. They are winning right now because the formula isn't breaking.
