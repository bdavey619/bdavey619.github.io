Game Driver

State of Play

Severino didn't just pitch seven scoreless innings. He made Kansas City irrelevant before the game was decided.

This was a team offense creating traffic and a starter turning traffic into runway. Ten hits, four strikeouts looking, and an ERA that dropped from 4.35 to 4.27. That's not noise. That's the formula resetting itself after a fragile stretch. The division is tight enough that a pitcher who gives you one earned run over seven innings is not a luxury. He's the ceiling. And when the lineup actually showed up to support him, the game was never going anywhere.

What to Watch

The pattern has become clear. They're not dominant. They're stable. Severino held down the middle of the order when it mattered. McNeil and Cortes touched the game, but this was decided by competence stacked on competence, not a single moment or carry job. That's the difference between a win that lingers and one that feels like found money. Good teams don't need fireworks to beat Kansas City at home.
