State of Play

Colorado took this game early and never let it slip. The Athletics scored four in the first, then watched the Rockies answer with two and never stop. Springs lasted four innings. Enough for the damage to compound when Luis Medina walked out and gave up six runs before anyone warmed up. It was a blowout by the fifth, which meant the offense's 15 hits and Soderstrom's home run added up to noise.

The bullpen hemorrhaged in the middle innings. Medina, Basso, and Scott Barlow combined to give up 14 runs across 4 innings. That is not a relief appearance; that is a surrender. Soderstrom's night showed the lineup could hit. He drove in two runs and proved the bats were ready. But Colorado's collapse in the field never mattered. The Rockies scored in five separate innings, built a lead that felt permanent by the fourth, and the Athletics chased from that point forward. A 15-hit performance means nothing when it spans nine innings and produces only nine runs. They had the chance early and folded it immediately.

What to Watch

Pittsburgh tomorrow at 6:40. Jared Jones took the mound for the Pirates with a 4.73 ERA this season. Not a wall, but not a gift either. J.T. Ginn gets the ball for Vegas and needs to do what Springs couldn't: eat innings without inviting disaster.
