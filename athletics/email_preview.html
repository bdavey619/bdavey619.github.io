State of Play

Late innings found them. Butler's swing in the ninth erased four runs and a night that almost slipped away.

They trailed through most of this game. Down four after six innings, nothing suggested a comeback was forming. The bullpen walked a tight rope. Krook gave away two runs in the first third of an inning, then Hartlieb and Barnett locked the door for two and one. That anchor held long enough for the lineup to catch up. McNeil went 3-for-4 with a hit that mattered. Bolte stayed hot at 3-for-5. Fifteen hits across nine at-bats does not leave runners stranded. They converted six of sixteen RISP situations into runs, which is production when it counted. The ninth inning was the payoff: four runs after six were tied, because Butler drove a single through the middle with two outs and the tying run on second. That was the moment the game flipped from survival to finish. This is a team that does not need early innings. Give them late at-bats and enough of their own players on base, and they will find a way to swing it.
