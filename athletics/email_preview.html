State of Play

The game got away in one inning and never came back.

Tampa Bay arrived in the second and scored two. The Athletics chased from that point forward, scoring only once across five RISP situations through the middle innings. Gelof hit for power twice. His home run in the third made it a one-run game briefly. But the offense couldn't string anything together. By the time they scratched out two runs in the eighth, they were down four and the game was already decided. The bullpen was clean behind Perkins's rough start, but they were the only part of this game that worked. Perkins gave up twelve hits and six runs in five innings. After that, Rom, Blewett, Juenger, and Johnson combined for four scoreless frames and three strikeouts. It didn't matter. The offense left two runners on in the first inning. A critical miss. And never recovered from falling behind early. This is the pattern again: they had chances and did nothing with them.

What to Watch

They are now 2-8 in their last ten games and 14.5 games back. The inconsistency that has haunted this season tightened its grip today. Early leads in the second and fourth innings went to Tampa, and the Athletics spent the rest of the night reaching but not arriving. Gelof's two home runs kept them in the game longer than they had any right to be, but one player's power doesn't finish games when the rest of the lineup goes quiet. They left three more on base after the first two innings and scored once. This is what losing looks like when the pitching does its job and the bats don't answer. Seven hits is not enough when six come after the game is over.
