State of Play

The pitching finally broke and took the game with it.

A 6–14 loss exposes something harder to hide now: 4.64 ERA is not a variance problem. It is the thing. Lopez lasted 5.1 innings and gave up six runs. The lineup kept answering. Langeliers with two home runs, McNeil contributing. But on the wrong side of a blowout, production becomes noise. Fourteen hits matter less when you allow fourteen runs. This is not a team that loses close games. This is a team that gets separated by the pitching it relies on not failing. When the starters can't hold, the whole math breaks. The staff is becoming the margin they cannot close.
