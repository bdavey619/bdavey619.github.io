State of Play

Jung Hoo Lee hit the go-ahead single in the ninth and it still wasn't enough.

They needed one more swing to finish it. That's the game. They didn't get it. Nine hits, 13 strikeouts. The offense created traffic and Lee turned some of it into runs, but the margin wasn't real. Houser gave them four innings and three earned runs before leaving, and Philadelphia answered. It's just getting louder. They had their chance in the ninth and couldn't make it hold.
