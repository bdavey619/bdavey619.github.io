Quotes

Things I keep coming back to.

  • "We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit."
    — Will Durant (summarizing Aristotle)
    The most misattributed quote in history, and still the best one on consistency.
  • "The best way to predict the future is to invent it."
    — Alan Kay
  • "Everybody has a plan until they get punched in the mouth."
    — Mike Tyson
    Applies to software projects more often than I'd like to admit.
  • "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a good deal of it."
    — Seneca
