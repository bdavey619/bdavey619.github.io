  • updated medium They trailed in every game of the series. They swept it anyway Oct 20–Oct 28 · 7×

    World Series Game 1 played Oct 23, 2026; Game 2 Oct 24 Milwaukee, Wisconsin / Tampa Bay, Florida

    The Milwaukee Brewers swept the two-time defending champion Dodgers in the NLCS for their first pennant since 1982, then won three straight to open the World Series over Tampa Bay - a franchise that has never won a title facing one that hasn't either.

    What does it mean to win every game of a series without ever actually being ahead until the moment that mattered?

    Oct 28 Game 3: Brewers 10, Rays 1. An eight-run fifth inning - a World Series record, built on seven straight hits - ended any suspense; Milwaukee takes a 3-0 series lead and can clinch the title with a win in Game 4.
