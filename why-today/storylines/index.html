  • medium This feathered dinosaur grew four wings. It's proof flight was invented twice Oct 4

    described Sept 29, 2026 Jiufotang Formation, Liaoning, China (Early Cretaceous)

    Norellraptor barsboldi, a crow-sized, four-winged feathered dinosaur described Sept 29 from a complete 57cm skeleton in northeastern China, belongs to a different group than its famous four-winged cousin Microraptor - evidence dinosaurs evolved flight-ready wings independently, more than once.

    What does it mean for a single solution - four feathered wings - to be invented twice by unrelated animals facing the same problem?

    Oct 4 The deeper analysis: about 30% of the anatomical changes on Norellraptor's family tree also appear in the lineage leading to birds - but acquired in a different sequence, arguing against a shared blueprint and for separate evolutionary pressures reaching the same answer.
