    Navratri begins Oct 11, 2026, runs nine nights to Dussehra Oct 20 India and the Hindu diaspora worldwide

    Shardiya Navratri begins today with Ghatasthapana: a sacred kalash installed as Durga's vessel, often packed with soil, coins and grain with barley seeds sown beneath it, opening nine nights devoted to nine successive forms of the goddess - the first being Shailaputri.

    What does it mean to represent one deity's power as nine distinct, separately-named forms rather than one continuous figure?

    Oct 14 Day four belongs to Kushmanda - credited in tradition with creating the universe with just her smile, and said to reside inside the sun itself, providing it the energy to shine. Royal blue is the day's colour; malpua the customary offering.

    3 earlier updates
    • Oct 13Day three belongs to Chandraghanta - named for the bell-shaped half-moon on her forehead, riding a tiger (or in some traditions a lion), and associated with courage rather than any of the gentler virtues the earlier forms carry. Milk or kheer is the customary offering.
    • Oct 12Day two belongs to Brahmacharini - not a married goddess but an ascetic one, the unmarried Parvati mid-penance, shown barefoot with a prayer rosary and a water pot instead of any of the weapons or finery the later forms carry. The same day is also Chandra Darshana, the first sighting of the new moon.
    • Oct 11Day one belongs to Shailaputri, the mountain's daughter - first of Durga's nine forms, associated in several traditions with the Muladhara (root) chakra, the base the other eight days are said to build on.
