    World Series Game 1 played Oct 23, 2026; Game 2 Oct 24 Milwaukee, Wisconsin / Tampa Bay, Florida

    The Milwaukee Brewers swept the two-time defending champion Dodgers in the NLCS for their first pennant since 1982, then took Game 1 of the World Series 3-2 over Tampa Bay - a franchise that has never won a title facing one that hasn't either.

    What does it mean to win every game of a series without ever actually being ahead until the moment that mattered?
