  • updated medium They trailed in every game of the series. Then the series stopped letting them win that way Oct 20–Oct 28 · 8×

    World Series Game 1 played Oct 23, 2026; Game 2 Oct 24 Milwaukee, Wisconsin / Tampa Bay, Florida

    The Milwaukee Brewers swept the two-time defending champion Dodgers in the NLCS for their first pennant since 1982, then took a 3-0 World Series lead over Tampa Bay before the Rays avoided the sweep - a title neither franchise has ever won.

    What does it mean to win every game of a series without ever actually being ahead until the moment that mattered?

    Oct 28 Game 4: Rays 4, Brewers 3 (12 innings). Cooper Pratt's homer put Milwaukee ahead in the top of the 12th; Tampa Bay answered with two in the bottom, Ben Williamson's hit walking it off. Series now 3-1; Milwaukee can still clinch in Game 5.
