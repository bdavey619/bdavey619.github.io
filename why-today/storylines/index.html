  • A Colorado county is suing oil companies for the climate. The Supreme Court just agreed to listen Oct 11–Oct 12 · 2×

    oral arguments heard Oct 5, 2026, opening day of the court's new term Washington, DC / Boulder County, Colorado

    The Supreme Court opened its new term Oct 5 with Suncor Energy v. Boulder County - the first case asking whether a county can sue oil companies under state law for climate damages, or whether federal law preempts claims over harm that crosses every border.

    Can a single county's lawsuit meaningfully assign blame for a problem caused by a global industry over a century?

    Oct 12 Suncor's lawyer gave the argument's starkest framing: if Boulder's claims proceed, some 90,000 U.S. municipalities could effectively set national and international energy policy through local jury verdicts - the scale critics say the case was always really about.

    1 earlier update
    • Oct 11A fuller read of the argument complicates the "sympathetic to oil companies" read: justices reportedly voiced doubts about both sides' broad positions, not just Boulder's - and the 4-4 deadlock risk cuts the other way too, since a tie would leave Colorado's ruling (and Boulder's lawsuit) standing by default.
